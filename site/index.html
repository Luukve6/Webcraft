Website editing for small businesses

Update your own website. Without the hassle.

Webcraft is the editor behind your website. Change a price on the menu, your opening hours or add a new page yourself – a minute later it’s live.

  • No WordPress, no plugins, no updates
  • Works on your computer, tablet and phone
  • Fast and secure, no cookies or tracking

What you can do yourself

Everything that changes often, you update yourself. The design of the site always stays tidy.

Menu and products

Add dishes or products, change prices and put categories in the right order.

Opening hours

Hours per day, public holidays and single closed days. The website shows by itself whether you’re open right now.

Holidays and notices

Enter the dates and a notice appears three weeks ahead – and disappears again afterwards.

Pages and photos

Create new pages, format text and add photos. Choose yourself what appears in the menu.

Downloads

Upload a new takeaway menu or flyer as a PDF. Visitors always get the latest one.

Several editors

Every team member logs in with their own account. Removing someone takes one click.

How it works

  1. Log inOne click on “Log in with GitHub”, at yoursite/admin.
  2. EditFill in the form. The editor checks your input – a date with the wrong year, or a closing time before the opening time.
  3. PublishClick “Save and publish”. Within a minute it’s on your website.

Why Webcraft

A website that just keeps working, without you having to look after it.

Fast
Your website consists of ready-made pages on a worldwide network. No slow server, no database.
Secure
There’s no server or database to hack, unlike WordPress. Only people you give access can change anything.
No maintenance
No updates, no plugins going out of date, no security holes to keep track of.
Everything saved
Every change is kept, with who made it and when. An older version can always be brought back.
